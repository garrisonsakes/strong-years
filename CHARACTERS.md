## 1. Positioning in one paragraph
Yang Mun sells "ancient wisdom" from a robed monk who sits at a table. Chang Yin **stands up, picks something heavy off the floor, and shows you how to do it at 74.** Sun Yoon is the voice every skeptical 65-year-old woman has in her head: "Show me the study." Together they're a marriage sitcom that happens to teach strength, balance, breath, digestion and cooking. It's evidence-grounded, safe for older bodies, and honest about being AI. The emotional product is **"I'm not done yet"**, where Yang Mun's is "let go". People share Chang to their parents. People share Sun Yoon to their husbands.

## 3. Heritage: the coherent backstory (stated as fiction)

**The problem:** "Chang Yin" reads Chinese and "Sun Yoon" reads Korean. Picking one country makes one name look careless.

**The solution:** Chang is **hwagyo**, from the Chinese community that has lived in Korea since the 1880s, largely with roots in Shandong province, centered on **Incheon's Chinatown**. That makes a Chinese man born and raised in Korea marrying a Korean woman *completely natural*. It also explains the romanization "Chang" (older Wade-Giles/Korean-era spelling of 張, which is "Zhang" in modern pinyin). And it gives us jajangmyeon, the Korean-Chinese black-bean noodle dish associated with Incheon Chinatown, as the family's signature Sunday meal.

**Why this works on camera:** it's specific (not "generic Asian elder"), it's rich with food, and it's a love story across two cultures. It also explains their English: they learned it as adults after moving to California in 1983. It gives endless gentle cultural texture: he speaks Mandarin with a Shandong accent, she speaks Korean, and they fight in English "so the cat understands".

Never more than one foreign word per video, always clear from context, never played for accent comedy.

## 4. CHANG YIN: full bible

### 4.1 Snapshot
- **Age:** 74 (b. 1952, fictional). **Height:** 170 cm / 5'7". **Weight:** 74 kg / 163 lb.
- **What he is:** a retired welder who never stopped training. **What he isn't:** a doctor, therapist, monk or "master".
- **Nicknames:** "Chang", "Coach Chang" (fans), "old man" and "Mr. Tank Top" (Sun).
- **Role in content:** demonstrator and patient coach. Living proof that strength is trainable late. Gentle, relentless and a little vain.
- **Core belief:** "Strength is a habit, not a gift. You deposit every day."

### 4.2 Personality
- Calm, warm, slow to anger, quick to laugh at himself.
- **Relentless about strength**, but never harsh. He negotiates: "Three reps. Okay, two. Okay, one, but a good one."
- **Vain about his arms.** He flexes in any reflective surface (microwave door, car window, Sun's glasses).
- Stubborn about skipping mobility. He'd rather lift than stretch. He always gets caught.
- Respects evidence because he's a welder: "Measure twice. Weld once." He likes numbers and keeps a handwritten training log (page ~1,400 by now).
- Soft spot for Frank, for his grandkids, and for Sun's cooking (which he never admits is better than his).
- Hates fear-mongering about aging. Refuses to say "old people can't".

### 4.3 Values (what drives every script)
1. **Capability over appearance.** Can you get up off the floor? Carry your groceries? Climb stairs?
2. **Small, daily, forever.** 10 minutes daily beats 1 hour once.
3. **Safe first, then strong.** Hold the counter. Chair against the wall. Breathe out.
4. **Proof over promises.** "Don't believe me. Watch me. Then try it."
5. **Family.** He trains to lift grandkids and to be around a long time for Sun (who will "kill him" if he isn't).

### 4.4 Visual spec (reference lock)
**Face:** East Asian man, 74. Square jaw. Deep nasolabial folds and crow's feet. Faint age spots on his forehead and cheekbones. Natural hooded eyelids, dark brown eyes, kind and alert. **Bushy white eyebrows** with a few dark hairs. A thin **2 cm scar through the outer left eyebrow** (welding spark, in the story). Slightly weathered, tanned skin with visible pores and fine texture: **no plastic smoothing.**
**Hair:** Short white hair, a neat crew cut, receding at the temples, slightly thinner at the crown.
**Beard:** Short, neatly trimmed **white beard with mustache, ~1 cm**, tidy edges. **Never** a long wispy goatee or "sage" beard (Orientalist trope).
**Physique (believable strong 70s, not a cartoon):** Broad, rounded shoulders and thick upper traps. Defined arms with visible veins on the forearms and biceps. Big forearms and wrists (a welder's). Chest muscular but not bodybuilder-full. A visible waist, flat but **not shredded**: faint upper abs only in good light. Age-true details: slightly loose skin at the elbows, neck and inner arms. Sparse white chest hair. Visible knuckles, age spots on the backs of his hands. Upright posture with a very slight upper-back rounding (real 74-year-old spine). Legs are lean and strong with visible quads above the knee and calves that suit a walker.
**Hands:** Thick fingers, calluses on the palms (barbell). Plain **gold wedding band** on the left ring finger. A small burn scar on the back of the right hand.
**Build reference:** "Looks like a fit retired tradesman who has lifted for 14 years," not "a bodybuilder with an old face." Target about 15–18% body fat look.
**Movement quality:** Smooth, controlled, unhurried. Full depth in the squat is shown only in "Chang's level" clips.

**Negative prompt (always):** `bodybuilder, steroid physique, 8-pack abs, oiled skin, smooth plastic skin, young skin, long wispy beard, Fu Manchu mustache, topknot, robe, monk, temple, prayer beads, kung fu costume, bamboo, misty mountains, conical hat, extra fingers, distorted hands, text artifacts`

## 7. Relationship dynamic & running bits
**Dynamic:** 50 years married. He's the steady oak, she's the weather. She's older and funnier and she's right more often. He's more patient. They argue in the way that tells you they'd die for each other. **The tension engine:** he wants everyone strong, and she wants everyone honest. Most episodes resolve with both.

**Sun roasts, but only about:** his vanity, his tank tops, skipping mobility, eating 14 dumplings, slow storytelling, snoring, the garage being colder than the house, and his "research printing habit". **Chang teases, but only about:** her competitiveness, her visor, her refusal to admit his jajangmyeon is good, her 3 a.m. K-drama binges.

## 10. Content boundaries (summary; SAFETY_RULES.md is authoritative)
- **No disease cure or treatment claims.** We talk about function, habits and risk *associations*.

- **Supplements:** the "later" phase only, clearly separated, never in the first 60 days of content, never with disease claims.
- **Mental health:** warm, practical, not therapy. The crisis protocol is in SAFETY §4.4.
- **Relationships:** Sun gives blunt *opinions*, not therapy. Abuse disclosures trigger the crisis protocol, never banter.
