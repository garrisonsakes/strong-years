How to read the tables:
- **Scripts available: 190 unique, not 230.** `runway_scripts.json` (40) is the S151–S190 subset of `scripts.json` (190) with extra fields. The IDs and text match, so they add nothing. All 190 are placed by D+26.
- **Generation backlog:** @changyin needs generated scripts from **D−5 (Sat Oct 3)**. Most of its runway library is movement work that has to wait for the D−3 shoot, so the pre-shoot slots need non-movement GEN scripts. With the 48 h brief lead, `tools/build_content.py` has to produce @changyin's first batch **tonight (D−7)**, @changandsun's by D−6, and @sunyoon.kitchen's by D0. From D+7 the network runs on generation: ~21/day at first, 45/day from D+37, **3,436 GEN scripts in total** by D+90.
- **Human review** at 20 s per judged post grows from 18 min/day (runway) to **90 min/day** at full network. That's on top of the always-human items (offer posts, MB-EX, reply videos, ORGANIC_ENGINE §6) and the crisis DMs.
- **Render load** at full network (D+37+): ~1,390 s of lip-sync, ~290 s of motion control and ~720 s of Veo inserts generated per day (before retakes), ~126 min of packaged output video and ~270 assembly CPU-min/day. One 8-vCPU assembly box is enough (COSTS.md).

## 4. Validation (`python3 tools/validate_plan.py`; `python3 -m pytest -q tools/test_validate_plan.py`)

The validator checks:
- no file used twice;
- each group stays on one page and covers all 6 platforms exactly once, with YouTube as a distinct cut;
- each library script is used once network-wide;
- cadence is exact for every page × platform × day (98 days × 7 pages × 6 platforms), and no page posts before its start day;
- the CTA rules in §1;
- no movement lane before D−2;
- every slot is inside 06:30–21:30 ET;
- no more than 2 posts per pillar per page per day.

Current result: **21,756 posts, 3,626 groups: OK**. The tests (10) cover the clean plan plus mutated plans: a duplicate file, script reuse, a cadence shortfall, a waitlist CTA after D0, an offer CTA in the runway, the offer cap and a movement post before the shoot.

## 5. Open items and conflicts (decide; the plan follows the brief except where noted)

1. **Cadence vs canon ramp.** CONTENT_SYSTEM §8.4 and ORGANIC_ENGINE §1.3 warm new accounts at 1–2 → 3 → 5–6/day over three weeks. The brief's 3 → 5 → 6 → 7 ramp starts higher, and ORGANIC_ENGINE §1.2 ("we never compress the ramp") treats that as restriction risk. Threads/X here also follow the video ramp (up to 7/day), while canon has 4–5 text posts.
2. **Espanol.** The brief's ramp would take the page to 7/day. Canon's Mode A pilot is 3/day. The plan keeps **3/day**; change `PAGES["@changyin.espanol"]` cap to `None` to follow the ramp instead.
3. **Pre-shoot @changyin.** The 13 GEN scripts for D−5…D−3 have to exist and pass the judge within 48 h. Two ways out: move the shoot earlier, or let those slots run fewer posts (the validator would then flag the cadence).
4. The IG Trial Reels, Stories and pinned posts in ORGANIC_ENGINE §1.3/§3.4–3.5 aren't counted here; they're extra posts.
