**Status:** DRAFT: attorney review required. This template has not been reviewed by counsel. Don't send or sign it until a licensed attorney in the governing-law state has reviewed and adapted it, including the state physical-therapy and dietetics practice-act points in §4.

**Purpose:** engage a licensed professional (physical therapist or registered dietitian) to review Strong Years exercise and nutrition content before publication. Signing this agreement (plus the credential check in §3 and the naming consent in §6) is what unlocks the reviewer-gated copy marked `[ONLY PUBLISH ONCE A SIGNED, CREDENTIALED REVIEWER EXISTS: …]` in FUNNEL.md, ADS.md, OFFER.md and BLITZ_OPS.md (app flag `REVIEWER_SIGNED=true`).

## 1. Services

1.1 **Scope.** Reviewer will review, before publication, the content Company submits in these categories (tick those that apply):
- ☐ Exercise sessions (scripts, cues, progressions, level/track assignments, safety and "stop" rules, demonstrations on video)
- ☐ 12-week programs
- ☐ Strength Age tests and scoring descriptions
- ☐ Recipes and nutrition guidance (protein, fiber, sodium, texture/soft-food versions, remedy evidence grades)
- ☐ Safety screening questions and "talk to your doctor" guidance
- ☐ Paid ads and organic scripts that show exercise technique or make a health-related statement
- ☐ Live Q&A appearances (see §8)

1.2 **What review means.** Reviewer checks that content is accurate, appropriately conservative for adults aged roughly 55–85 exercising at home without supervision, consistent with generally accepted professional guidance, and free of claims that content diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents a disease. Reviewer either **approves**, **approves with required changes**, or **rejects** each item.

1.3 **Turnaround.** Reviewer will respond within `[2]` business days of submission for up to `[N]` items per week. Rush reviews (under 24 hours) by agreement at the rate in §5.

1.4 **Not clinical care.** Reviewer does not examine, diagnose, treat or advise individual members, does not form a clinician-patient relationship with any member, and is not responsible for any member's individual exercise decisions. Company will not present Reviewer as the member's clinician.

## 2. Approval record (reviewer-gate evidence log)

2.1 Every decision is recorded in Company's review log with: the asset ID, the exact version reviewed (file hash or version number), the date and time, the decision, the required changes, and Reviewer's electronic signature.

2.2 **Only the approved version may carry a review claim.** If Company changes an approved asset in any way that affects exercise instructions, safety cues, nutrition values or health statements, the review claim is removed until Reviewer approves the new version. Cosmetic changes (layout, captions' line breaks, brand colors) don't require re-review.

2.3 Company keeps the log for at least 5 years after the last publication of each asset and gives Reviewer read access to entries about Reviewer's own work.

## 3. Credentials

3.1 Reviewer represents that Reviewer holds, and will keep in good standing, the licence(s): `[LICENCE TYPE, STATE, NUMBER, EXPIRY]`; and, if applicable, board certification `[e.g. Geriatric Clinical Specialist]`; NPI `[NUMBER]`.

3.2 Company will verify the licence on the state board's public lookup before the first publication of any review claim, and again every `[6]` months. Company keeps a dated screenshot or record.

3.3 Reviewer will tell Company within 5 business days of any suspension, restriction, disciplinary action, lapse or investigation affecting the licence. Company may then suspend all review claims immediately.

## 4. Professional-practice points (counsel to adapt per state)

4.1 Counsel to confirm that content review for a consumer education product is not the practice of physical therapy or dietetics under the practice act of Reviewer's licensing state and the states where members live, and whether any disclosure to members is required.

4.2 Reviewer will not use Reviewer's patients' information, records or contact details in connection with Strong Years, and will not recommend Strong Years to patients in exchange for any benefit unless a separate, counsel-approved affiliate agreement covers fee-splitting, Anti-Kickback Statute and state rules (BLITZ_OPS.md §5).

4.3 Strong Years is never billed to Medicare, Medicaid or any insurer, and Reviewer will not imply otherwise.

## 5. Fees

5.2 Reviewer is an independent contractor, responsible for Reviewer's own taxes. Reviewer provides a W-9 before the first payment.

5.3 Fees don't depend on Reviewer approving any particular content, on sales, or on member results.
