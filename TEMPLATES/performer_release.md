# Performer Likeness, Motion-Capture and Digital Replica Release (template)

**Status:** DRAFT: attorney review required. This template has not been reviewed by counsel. Digital replica law is changing fast: California Labor Code §927 (AB 2602, effective Jan 1 2025) makes a digital-replica provision unenforceable unless it gives a reasonably specific description of the intended uses **and** the performer was represented by counsel or a union in the negotiation; New York has a similar rule for digital replica contracts (a 2024 amendment to its General Obligations Law; counsel to cite the current section); Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act applies if face geometry or other biometric identifiers are captured; and a union performer (SAG-AFTRA) is covered by its AI terms. Counsel must adapt this form per performer and per state **before** any capture.

**Purpose:** cover real people whose movement, body, voice or likeness is recorded for Strong Years: movement performers whose motion drives AI renders of the characters Chang Yin and Sun Yoon, and on-camera demonstrators (for example the real person who demonstrates easier versions as "Frank", and any picture-in-picture demonstrator in sessions).
