## 1. What the Affiliate does

1.1 Affiliate may recommend Strong Years using the referral link `https://[DOMAIN]/go?ref=[CODE]` and the code `[CODE]` assigned by Company on approval.

1.2 Affiliate is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent or partner of Company, and may not make commitments on Company's behalf.

## 2. Commission

2.1 **Rate and term.** Company pays **30%** of the amounts actually received from a Referred Customer, net of refunds, chargebacks, taxes and payment-processor reversals, for **12 months** from the Referred Customer's first order. Renewal charges within the 12 months count. Gift purchases do not.

2.2 **Referred Customer.** A person whose first Strong Years order (a) carries the Affiliate's code, or (b) is placed within **60 days** after they clicked the Affiliate's referral link, and in either case is not the Affiliate, a member of the Affiliate's household, or an account the Affiliate controls. One referral per customer; the first qualifying order decides which affiliate is credited.

2.3 **Hold and reversals.** A commission becomes payable 14 days after the charge (the membership's money-back window). Commission on a charge that is later refunded, charged back or disputed is reversed, including by deduction from a later payment.

2.4 **Statements and payment.** Company issues a monthly statement (CSV) by the 10th of the following month and pays payable amounts above `$[MINIMUM, e.g. 25]` by `[METHOD]` within `[N]` days. Amounts below the minimum roll over.

2.5 **Buyer price.** The Affiliate's code carries exactly the public starter terms. Nothing the Affiliate does changes the price a buyer pays, and Affiliate may not offer rebates, cash back or anything of value to buyers.

## 3. Disclosure (required every time)

3.1 Affiliate must clearly and conspicuously disclose the material connection **every time** a link or code is shared, close to the link or code and before any "more" cut, in the same medium (spoken in video and audio, on screen in video, in text in posts). Suggested wording: *"I'm a Strong Years affiliate and earn a commission if you join through my link or code. Your price is the same either way."* Hashtags alone (#ad, #affiliate) at the end of a long caption are not enough.

3.2 Affiliate must also use each platform's own paid-partnership or branded-content tool where one exists.

## 4. What Affiliate must never do

- Say or imply that Chang Yin or Sun Yoon are real people. They are AI characters, and every post that shows them must say so.
- Make any health, medical, cure, weight-loss, disease or outcome claim, or any claim about Strong Years that is not on Company's own pages. No before/after claims. No testimonials that are not the Affiliate's own honest experience.
- Use fake urgency, invented scarcity, countdowns, or seat counts that are not the live count on Company's page.
- Bid on, or run paid ads using, "Strong Years", "Chang Yin", "Sun Yoon" or misspellings, or run paid ads that link directly to Company's pages, without written permission.
- Post codes to coupon, deal or cash-back sites; use cookie stuffing, forced clicks, iframes, pop-unders, bots, incentivized sign-ups, or any traffic that is not a real person choosing to click.
- Send unsolicited email, DMs or texts; email only people who opted in to the Affiliate's own list, with CAN-SPAM-compliant identification and unsubscribe.
- Target or knowingly refer anyone under 18.
- Refer themselves, their household, or accounts they control.

## 5. Monitoring, suspension and termination

5.1 Company monitors referral patterns (self-referral, unusual volume, refund and chargeback rates) and may hold payments while it reviews them.

5.2 Either party may end this Agreement at any time by email. Company may suspend or terminate immediately for a breach of §3 or §4, in which case unpaid commissions on referrals obtained in breach are forfeited. Commissions earned in good standing before termination are paid on the normal schedule.

## 6. Brand use

Company grants a limited, revocable, non-exclusive licence to use the Strong Years name and the materials Company provides, unchanged, only to promote Strong Years under this Agreement. No other licence is granted. Affiliate may not register domains or social handles containing Company's names.

## 7. Data and privacy

Company does not share Referred Customers' personal information with Affiliate. Statements show order references, dates and amounts only.
