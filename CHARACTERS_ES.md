## 1. Market brief (research, cited; [R#] = §16)

### 1.1 Platforms (US Hispanic adults; 55+ where available)
- **WhatsApp is the Hispanic-specific channel.** 54% of Hispanic adults use WhatsApp (Pew, Jan 2024) [R1]; Latinos used WhatsApp far more than White Americans (53% vs 14%) and leaned on social media for health information (53% relied on it "a lot" for COVID-19 information vs 34% of White respondents) [R4]. Videos are the bulk of what gets forwarded [R3]. **Use:** the free WhatsApp **Channel** for top-of-funnel and "forward this to your mom" copies; never daily paid content by WhatsApp marketing templates (EXPANSION §5.7 cost note).
- **Facebook and YouTube are the 55+ platforms; TikTok is not (yet).** Only 10% of US adults 65+ use TikTok (Pew, Jan 2024) [R1]; the 2025 Pew update keeps the same age gap [R2]. Hispanic adults overall are heavy TikTok and Instagram users (49% and 58%) [R1], which is where their adult children are. **Use:** Facebook Reels + YouTube Shorts lead for the 55+ viewer; Instagram and TikTok reach the adult child who buys the gift (FAMILIA).
- **Instagram:** the canon roster's home (4 IG pages); the Spanish page is IG + FB + YT + TikTok + Threads + X like every page (CANON UPDATE 4: one render, reposted).

### 1.2 Health priorities, and where the no-disease-claims line sits
| Priority | Data | What we make | What we never make |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diabetes** (the elephant in the room) | Diagnosed diabetes: Hispanic adults 11.7% vs non-Hispanic White 6.9% (2019–2021) [R5]; older Mexican Americans carry far higher rates (24.9% of those 65+ diagnosed in 1999–2002; 37.2% of those 75+ in 2004–05 in the Hispanic EPESE cohort) [R6] | Habits any doctor would sign: walking after meals (E23, framed as a habit, no glucose numbers in prominent fields), protein at breakfast (E28), fiber from beans and nopales (E24), strength (E01). ### 1.3 Who already owns attention (the Spanish 55+ health field)
- **Physician communicators:** Dr. Élmer Huerta (Peruvian oncologist and public-health specialist, first Latino president of the American Cancer Society, *Hablemos de Salud*, CNN en Español) [R12]; Dr. Juan Rivera (Univision medical contributor; YouTube/IG/Facebook) [R13]. - **1952:** Jesús "Chuy" Arriaga born in Fresnillo, Zacatecas.
- **1954:** María Guadalupe Sandoval born in Guadalajara, Jalisco ("tapatía, y se nota").

- **Note on migration:** the lore says they "came to Los Angeles young" and stops there. Status, *papeles* and border stories are never content (§10).

## 4. DON CHUY: full bible

### 4.1 Snapshot
- **Name:** Jesús Arriaga ("Don Chuy"; Lupe calls him "Jesús" when scolding, "Chuy" when sweet). **Age:** 74 (b. 1952, fictional). **Height:** 172 cm / 5'8". **Weight:** 77 kg / 170 lb.
- **What he is:** retired *albañil* (40 years laying block and brick), father, grandfather, the family's proof that strength is trainable. **What he isn't:** a coach with credentials, a doctor, a *sobador*, a *huesero*, a healer.
- **Role in content:** strength, carries, chair and floor practice, balance, walking after meals, the patio garden; the calm demonstrator.
- **Core belief:** "La fuerza se entrena, no se hereda."

### 4.2 Personality
- Calm, slow, warm; a laugh that starts in the chest. Takes Lupe's roasts with a mustache twitch.
- **Vain about two things:** his forearms and his mustache (he grooms it in the kitchen window; see bit 9).
- Practical builder's mind: everything is *mezcla, block y nivel* ("mix, block and level"), which Lupe can't stand (bit 13).
- Patient teacher: shows the easy version first and calls it "donde empezamos, no donde nos quedamos".
- Private softness: the 2014 floor moment, his compadre Frank's knee, missing his brothers in Zacatecas. This is his comment engine.

### 4.3 Values
1. **Despacio, pero diario.** Consistency beats intensity.
2. **La pared cerca, siempre.** Safety is part of strength, not the opposite.
3. **Las piernas cargan la casa.** Legs first; carrying groceries is training.
4. **El que enseña, primero hace.** He shows it before he asks you.
5. **La familia se levanta junta.** Train with your kids, not for them.

### 4.4 Visual spec (reference lock)
**Face:** Mexican man, 74. Weathered tan-brown skin with sun damage on the forehead and cheekbones, deep smile lines, crow's feet, a broad nose, warm dark-brown eyes, heavy white eyebrows. **Thick white mustache, neatly trimmed** (signature), clean-shaven otherwise with light white stubble at the jaw. Short white hair, side-combed, slightly receding. Natural skin texture with pores, **no smoothing**.
**Build:** broad shoulders, strong forearms and hands of a bricklayer (thick fingers, calluses, a healed scar across the left thumb knuckle), visible deltoids, a small belly he doesn't hide, lean but not shredded. Slight forward head posture.
**Accessories (signature):** gold wedding band on the left hand; reading glasses in the shirt pocket; a black digital watch with a cracked strap.
**Expressions library:** the mustache twitch (suppressing a laugh), the slow nod, the eyebrow lift at Lupe, the chest laugh, the "a ver" squint at the camera.

**Negative prompt (always):** `sombrero, charro costume, poncho, serape, mariachi outfit, bandolier, cartoon mustache, caricature, bodybuilder, steroid physique, oiled skin, smooth plastic skin, young skin, gold chains, cowboy hat, extra fingers, distorted hands`

### 4.6 Voice & speech patterns
- **Spanish:** neutral Mexican-US Spanish with a light northern-central (Zacatecas) cadence: unhurried, clear, warm baritone. Target 130–140 wpm.
- **Register:** *usted* to viewers (A/B *tú* in the first 50 scripts); "mijo/mija" affectionately; "mi reina" or "Lupita" to his wife. **Never "vieja"** until the cultural reviewers clear it (§14).
- **Structure:** Statement. Demo. Cue. Easy version. Number from a study. Doable invitation.
- **Sentence length:** 4–10 words. Pauses carry the warmth.
- **Regional word choices:** *el mandado* (groceries), *la cochera* (garage), *la banqueta* (sidewalk), *la cubeta* (bucket), *el costal* (sandbag), *ahorita*, *a ver*, *fíjese*, *ándele* (max once). US units for loads and furniture: *libras*, *pulgadas*; *gramos* for protein. At most one everyday US loanword per video (*la troca*, *el lonche*), only where natural.
- **Signature phrases (max 1 per video):**

  3. "Con la pared cerca, siempre."
  4. "Las piernas cargan la casa."
  5. "Donde empezamos, no donde nos quedamos."
  6. "Saque el aire cuando cueste."
  7. "La silla no es su enemiga. Es su jefa." (see bit 2)

  9. "El orgullo no es barandal. Agárrese."
  10. "El músculo es una alcancía."
- **Openers:** "A ver." / "Fíjese en esto." / "Mire." / "Siéntese. Ahora levántese." / "Le voy a enseñar algo de albañil."
- **Closers:** "Despacio, pero diario." / "Y si Lupe dice que no, es que no." / "Mándeselo a su compadre."

## 5. DOÑA LUPE: full bible

### 5.1 Snapshot

- **Role in content:** kitchen and protein, myth-busting the forwarded remedies, blunt truths, family and loneliness, the voice of the skeptical viewer, Chuy's editor.
- **Core belief:** "Primero la verdad, luego el pan dulce."

### 5.2 Personality
- Quick, dry, affectionate scolding. Says the hard thing, then serves you a plate.
- **Skeptical by instinct:** "¿Y el estudio?" She reads Chuy's printed study card and circles the number of people in it.
- **Allergic to WhatsApp remedies:** the forwarded audios from comadre Toña are her favorite enemy (bit 3).
- Competitive: her balance time beats his and it's on the kitchen calendar (bit 5).
- Generous to a fault: feeds the block, remembers every birthday, calls Marisol every Sunday.
- Private softness: her mother's kitchen in Guadalajara, closing the fonda, friends who have passed. This is the emotional engine of the comments.

### 5.3 Values
1. **La verdad es cariño.** She'd rather hurt your feelings than your knees.
2. **Gramos, no cuentos.** Food is love and also arithmetic.
3. **La familia es salud** (E37): call someone, invite someone to eat.
4. **Envejecer con dignidad.** "No estoy contra la edad. Estoy a favor de vivir."

## 10. Never-say list and content boundaries (SAFETY_RULES.md is authoritative; prompts/blocked_claims.json BC26–BC37 enforce the Spanish part)

### 10.1 Never say (any character, any field)
- **Cure and disease language:** *cura, curar, sana(r) + condition, revierte/revertir, elimina/adiós a + condition, milagro, milagroso*.
- **The diabetes line:** *baja/controla/regula/normaliza el azúcar, la glucosa, la diabetes; insulina vegetal; "para la diabetes" next to any food or drink*.
- **Blood pressure, cholesterol, joints, menopause:** *baja la presión, quita la artritis, desinflama las rodillas, quita los bochornos*.
- **Medication:** *deje / suspenda / cambie su medicina, sus pastillas o la insulina; "ya no va a necesitar"; sin medicamentos*.
- **Wellness nonsense:** *desintoxica, limpia el hígado / el colon / la sangre, toxinas, superalimento* (except mocked), *dióxido de cloro, CDS, MMS*.
- **Conspiracy and secrecy:** *lo que la farmacia / los doctores no te dicen, secreto de la abuela, remedio ancestral*.
- **Credentials and healers:** *doctor/a, enfermera, nutrióloga, fisio, terapeuta, curandero/a, sobador, huesero, yerbera, maestro, chamán, monje, templo* (as self-claims or authority).
- **Offer words:** *garantía* except in "garantía de devolución de 14 días" (never next to a health result); **"de por vida"** and "para toda la vida" (the price is "bloqueado mientras sigas suscrito"); *gratis* only where something is actually free; *últimos lugares, quedan pocos, apúrate, termina hoy*.
- **Speed and weight:** *al instante, en minutos, de inmediato* + result; *quema grasa, baja X kilos/libras*.
- **Body and people:** *gordita/gordo*, any body or weight comment about a viewer; **"vieja"** (pending reviewers); mocking accents or *pochos*; jokes about drinking.
- **Never content:** immigration, *papeles*, *la migra*, deportation, politics, elections, religion performed on camera (no saints, no "Dios te va a curar"; incidental *gracias a Dios* in a heartfelt line only with reviewer sign-off), alcohol as health, fasting, raw-egg tonics.

### 10.2 The diabetes line, operational
- **Acknowledge, never treat.** Allowed: "Muchos de ustedes viven con diabetes. Aquí no la tratamos. Hablamos de hábitos que su médico firmaría."
- **Standing skip line** for any food, walking-after-meals or fasting-adjacent script: "¿Usa insulina o pastillas para el azúcar? Pregúntele a su médico antes de cambiar su comida."
- **Prominent fields** (hook line, on-screen text, thumbnail, titles) never name a condition; hashtags never name a condition (Spanish stems added to the build-time hashtag check).
- **Evidence framing:** E23 (walking after meals) is framed as a habit ("caminar dos minutos después de comer"), never with glucose numbers in prominent fields and never with "diabetes" in the same sentence.

### 10.3 Movement and food rules (same as the flagship)
Support cue, easier version, stop rule and breathing cue in every movement video; chair against the wall; no breath-holds; no loaded spinal flexion. Food scripts carry a "quién debe saltarse esto" line when relevant (kidney disease and protein; low-salt diets; insulin users).
