# ENGINE_100X.md: evidence-ranked improvements to the Strong Years organic engine

Research round of Oct 2 2026. I ran about 60 web searches and fetches and read BRIEF.md (CANON UPDATE 2–4), VIRALITY_SYSTEM.md, STACK_FINAL.md, ORGANIC_ENGINE.md §0–3 and workers/README.md. I also read `workers/growth/adapters.py`, `workers/uniqueness/guard.py` and CONTENT_SYSTEM §8 to tie each item to real code. No spending, accounts, posting or generation calls were made.

**How to read this.** Each layer has 10 items, ranked by expected impact on reach → owned contacts → MRR. Each item gives What · Why (evidence) · How (file or worker) · Cost · Confidence (H/M/L). Sources are tagged `[S#]` and listed in §12. **[M]** means our own POSTDB data, **[A]** means an assumption that still needs a test, and **[V-]** means the source is a vendor or a secondary write-up rather than the platform's own documentation.

**Context assumed:** 4 IG pages plus their Facebook Pages (Facebook is a primary channel), reposted to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Threads and X. 25 masters/day are multiplied into 150–250 posts. Shopify membership. No collabs. No paid cold ads at launch.

**Three findings change existing canon. Read these first.**
1. **The modular multiplier, as worded, is the biggest reach risk in the plan.** I found **no public evidence** that hook-swapped variants of one body perform as distinct posts in organic feeds. Meta's 2026 originality rules demote low-value edits account-wide [S3]. Instagram's Apr 30 2026 repost crackdown removes accounts that repost from recommendations [S20, V-]. On the ads side, Meta's retrieval reportedly clusters near-identical creatives into one entity [S44, V-]. YouTube's July 2026 rules call template-based content with minimal variation "inauthentic" [S26]. **So the 150–250 count should come from (master × platform) placements plus Trial-Reel tests, not from same-platform duplicates** (§5).
2. **Our YouTube score is now measuring the wrong thing.** Since Aug 27 2026 YouTube counts a view the moment playback starts, replays included. Only `engagedViews` keeps the old meaning [S27]. `adapters.py` still asks for `views`.
3. **TikTok cuts reach on posts that send people to buy outside TikTok Shop** (guidelines effective Sep 13 2025) [S32]. TikTok scripts that end with "BOOK" or a price have to change.
