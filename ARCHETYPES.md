# ARCHETYPES.md: 10 archetypes for new pages and languages

Each archetype reuses the **Chang & Sun machine** unchanged: the same pillars, formats (F01–F37), SAFETY_RULES.md, EVIDENCE.md IDs, funnel and pipeline. Only the **bible, sets, voice, food and language layer** changes. Every archetype is **openly AI**, **not clergy or a healer**, and **not a credentialed persona** (no fictional doctors, nurses, physios, yogis-as-gurus or herbalists). Authority comes from the body on screen, real studies and [ONLY PUBLISH ONCE A SIGNED, CREDENTIALED REVIEWER EXISTS: a named human review team] FALLBACK: "a real team that builds every video on published guidelines (no credential claims)", the same as the flagship.

## How to clone (the checklist the pipeline follows)
1. **Bible:** copy CHARACTERS.md §4/§5 structure: snapshot, personality, values, visual spec + negative prompt, 5–6 wardrobe sets, voice card, signature phrases (≤12), "never says" list, 8 running bits, 1–2 side characters, and a fictional timeline stated as fiction.
2. **Home world:** 6–8 sets from their real everyday life. No temples, shrines, costumes or tourist-postcard backdrops.
3. **Language:** scripts are written natively by the writer LLM in the target language, with a native-speaker reviewer for the first 50 scripts, then spot checks. Never line-by-line translation.
4. **Evidence:** reuse E-IDs. Add local guideline sources where claims are localized (e.g., the national dietary guideline). Local health-advertising law check: DE HWG/HCVO, BR ANVISA/CONAR, IN ASCI/DMR Act, JP Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Act, EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency.
5. **Disclosure strings:** corner tag, bio, caption footer and pinned "we're AI" post, localized (template in SAFETY §7).
6. **Keywords:** 8 core keywords localized, mapped to the same DM flows (translated + reviewed).
7. **Cultural review:** 2 paid consultants from the community (credited). A "caricature audit" of 10 random videos every 2 weeks.
8. **Price localization** per OFFER.md's price-test cells, adjusted to purchasing power. Payment rails: Pix (BR), UPI (IN), SEPA (EU), konbini/credit (JP).
